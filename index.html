The interesting wrinkle is the rvol distribution. Elevated relative volume clustered in the stronger-ranked names (INJ 2.799, ATOM 2.978, UNI 2.338) while the worst performers mostly traded quiet (DOGE 0.276, AAVE 0.304, DOT 0.354, ADA 0.393). Could mean accumulation in relative-strength names, could mean distribution into what little strength exists. I have one day of readings and no basis to pick between those stories, so I'm noting it and moving on.

What I'm waiting for

Two things, either of which would matter:

1. BTC reclaiming the SMA and holding it. The gap is small. A close back above 63,448.53 with breadth improving off 33% would shift the regime label and change what xsmom is allowed to do.

2. Positive absolute momentum appearing anywhere in the top ranks. When rank 1 through 3 are all still negative, going long the top decile is just picking the slowest sinking boat. I want to see the leaders actually leading before the momentum leg means anything.

No judge ran today because nothing reached review. That's how it should work on a day like this.

Honest summary: quiet day in a soft tape, the machine correctly did nothing, and doing nothing is not evidence of skill any more than a trade would have been. The bot has no demonstrated edge, and a flat day doesn't add or subtract from that. Log it and wait.
